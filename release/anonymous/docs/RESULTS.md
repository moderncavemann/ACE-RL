that the comparator has worse low-risk customer execution than the target.
Confidence intervals are policy-seed 95% t intervals unless labeled as paired
two-way bootstrap intervals.

## Selectivity And Economic Interpretation

Aligned and exact-random soft-rank both announce a contract on exactly 20.10%
of decisions. In high-risk states, aligned announces on 9.42% of decisions and
uses mean routing TV 0.0292, compared with 19.44% and 0.0597 for exact random.
This selectivity improves the preregistered low-risk endpoint but does not
minimize all-state spread: aligned all-state spread is 6.3511 versus 6.3420 for
exact random.

Relative to exact random, aligned routing also has higher aggregate dealer
reward (9378.24 versus 9339.19), lower inventory SD (8.90 versus 9.42), and
lower maximum absolute inventory (39.73 versus 40.80). These are component
outcomes, not a total-welfare claim. Current revelation improves low-risk
execution over lagged revelation but lowers aggregate dealer reward by about
56 units, so it is not presented as a costless Pareto improvement.

The 0.03996-tick low-risk effect is 0.63% relative to exact random and saves
47.92 low-risk customer tick-units per episode. High-risk announced exposure
falls by 51.6%, aggregate dealer reward is 39.05 units higher, and inventory SD
is 5.6% lower. Aligned all-state spread is 0.0091 ticks worse, so these values
support selective improvement rather than all-state or total-welfare dominance.

Identity permutation is the strongest channel diagnostic: it preserves the
allocation-weight multiset and similar routing TV but changes weight-quote
rank correlation from 0.893 to -0.028 and returns low-risk spread close to the
no-routing reference. Arbitrary redistribution is therefore insufficient;
the quote-allocation mapping matters.

## Proxy Validation And Frozen Cost Profile

The final public-risk proxy uses only lagged public volatility and absolute
lagged return at runtime. On disjoint passive panels of 1,000 training and
1,000 validation observations, its simulator-supervised low-risk AUC is 0.8284;
volatility-only and return-only AUCs are 0.7573 and 0.7976. Latent simulator
risk is an offline label and post-hoc stratum, not a runtime input. Spread-only
is an outcome comparator, not a risk classifier.

The non-outcome efficiency profile uses the formal ABCE `linux/amd64` Docker
image with one CPU/thread, fixed `N=10`, and 100,000 measured calls per module.
Median / p95 / p99 contract control-path latency is 230.45 / 244.77 / 280.50
microseconds. The announced no-routing path is 161.28 / 176.69 / 207.55
microseconds, so the median routing increment is 69.18 microseconds (1.43x).
The 560-parameter actor adapter yields 6,257 response-trainable parameters when
the critic is included. Peak process RSS is 67.97 MB. These measurements ran
under Docker architecture emulation on Apple Silicon; they are not complete
ABIDES-step or native production latency and do not support a negligible
overhead claim.

## Retained Boundaries

- At target dose 0.10, matched random beats risk-aware under the policy-seed
  t interval: -0.00463 [-0.00757, -0.00169], 1/10 positive. The two-way
  bootstrap interval crosses zero. This retained cell rules out uniform
  dominance across doses.
- The forced-undercut diagnostic finds no supported rival spread compression,
  continuation flow loss, or continuation reward loss; 96.25% of high-spread
  episodes recover by the fixed horizon. The label is
  `high_spread_coordination_only`, not punishment-supported collusion.
- The Brero-style threshold buy-box and Banerjee-style temporary buyer
  commitment are ABIDES adaptations. Frozen convergence and/or budget-matching
  gates failed, so their efficacy outcomes are descriptive only. The artifact
  does not claim superiority over the original literature methods.

## Supported Claim

> In a controlled N=10 ABIDES dealer market, aligning a fixed
> announced-contract multiset with lagged public market state improves
> low-risk customer execution relative to exact-multiset random, circularly
> shifted, and periodic schedules after dealer adaptation. Timely
> current-contract revelation outperforms one-step-lagged revelation, and
> quote-aligned allocation outperforms identity-permuted redistribution. The
> alignment effect also transfers to the tested hard-best allocation contract
> and has a positive policy-seed mean effect under the tested exact risk-only
> full-policy MAPPO implementation. IPPO remains inconclusive.

The method does not minimize all-state raw spread. The evidence does not
establish universal learner, population-size, matching-engine, or real-market
robustness.
