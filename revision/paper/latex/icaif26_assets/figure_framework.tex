\caption{Risk-aware routing and information timing. Latent simulator risk is
used only to fit the public-risk proxy and to stratify outcomes post hoc. At
runtime, the platform computes and announces $u_t$ from lagged public history
before dealers quote; only then does it use current quotes to reallocate a
bounded share of fixed customer volume through quote-sensitive routing.
Exact-random, circular, and periodic schedules isolate state alignment;
one-step-lagged revelation isolates current contract visibility; identity
permutation and no routing isolate the quote--allocation channel; hard-best
tests a second allocation family. All arms retain paired step-level records.}
\label{fig:framework}
\Description{The diagram separates offline proxy calibration from runtime
information flow. At runtime, lagged public state produces a pre-quote signal,
dealers quote after observing it, and post-quote routing reallocates fixed
customer volume through ABIDES. Alignment, revelation, and allocation controls
feed into a paired audit layer.}
