# ABIDES/MAPPO Full Main v1

Status: completed; primary timing comparison passed  
Date: 2026-07-12

## Decision

The frozen 20-seed N=10 full-main experiment completed without seed failures
and passed its preregistered primary timing comparison. Learned risk-aware
timing reduced low-risk customer execution half-spread relative to both
equal-dose timing controls:

- matched random minus learned: 0.10893, 95% CI [0.09175, 0.12610], 20/20
  seeds positive;
- circular shuffled minus learned: 0.11137, 95% CI [0.09541, 0.12732],
  20/20 seeds positive.

The supported claim is:

> In the controlled N=10 ABIDES informed-flow dealer market, with a frozen
> no-intervention-trained MAPPO seller policy, learned risk-aware routing
> timing improves low-risk customer execution quality over equal-dose random
> and shuffled timing while conserving demand and keeping intervention
> selective.

## Interpretation

The learned schedule and both timing controls used identical aggregate and
risk-stratified activation rates. The learned arm nevertheless produced lower
low-risk execution half-spread on every seed. This identifies value in
state-contingent timing rather than intervention dose alone.

Routing is the causal channel: the no-routing arm equals no intervention on
every seed despite retaining the learned activation schedule. Mean volume is
exactly 20 in every arm, so the effect is not generated by adding or
withholding customer demand.

The learned gate is selective: mean activation is 0.248 and the per-seed range
is 0.13-0.35. Mean reward is 3.22% below no intervention, within the frozen 10%
guardrail. Spread-only threshold and always-on achieve slightly lower raw
execution half-spread, but activate substantially more often in high-risk
windows and have lower reward. The learned method's result is therefore a
timing and intervention-trade-off result, not a lowest-raw-spread result. The
oracle diagnostic remains better, showing remaining timing headroom.

## Claim Boundary

This is a completed full-main timing experiment in a controlled ABIDES dealer
market using a frozen MAPPO seller policy. It is not the canonical RMSC04
background-agent ecology, adaptive MAPPO retraining under each intervention
arm, evidence for all market sizes, or evidence that the learned gate attains
the lowest possible raw spread. Those require separate experiments and must
not be implied by this result.
